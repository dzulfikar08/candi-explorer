# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome newcomers, and assume good faith. Harassment,
personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, or any
other project space.

This includes how we discuss the monuments themselves. Candi are living heritage and, for many
people, sacred sites; discuss their religious and cultural context with care.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **dzulfikar.at@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.
The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block participants who violate this code, as
described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
